# Doctrine notes (design constraints, not legal advice)

- *Twombly* asks for factual context making agreement plausible; it sets no probability threshold. So the tool exposes connections and gaps and never scores them.
- Agreement and market context are separate tracks. Counsel selects the framework (per se, rule of reason, alternative, unresolved); merger-style market analysis is not imposed on a naked price-fixing theory (*Alston*).
- Allegations are assessed together but attributed defendant by defendant; affiliation is not participation (*SD3*).
- Plus-factor categories are evidentiary questions. "Contrary to unilateral interest" requires counsel to state the independent strategy, commercial facts and assumptions.
- Association contact is a ladder: membership, possible attendance, documented attendance, relevant discussion, alleged commitment, subsequent conduct.
- Standing, antitrust injury, limitations, jurisdiction and class issues sit in a separate register.
- The Merger Guidelines' market tools are agency guidance; the economist handoff carries data and assumptions, never generated estimates.
- `data/authority_manifest.json` holds paraphrased working notes marked unreviewed. Verify every entry and its current treatment before relying on it.
