# Data

- `tests/fixtures/synthetic/`: invented test material, bannered as such, generated by `scripts/make_synthetic_fixtures.py`. Not evidence of any real market.
- `data/authority_manifest.json`, `data/authorities/`: public legal authorities only, physically separate from fixtures.
- `data/taxonomies/`, `data/jurisdiction_profiles/`, `data/schemas/`: controlled vocabularies, circuit profiles, the internal decision-request contract.
- Client documents, databases, exports, credentials and model traces are git-ignored and must stay out of the repository.

Transactions keep `original` (verbatim row) and `norm` (computed) side by side; the mapping and formula are stored on the `import` object. Units convert to pounds. Net price exists only when every formula component is present.
